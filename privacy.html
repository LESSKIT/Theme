SUPPORT / PRIVACY

Your support request.

What you send

The support form collects your name, email address, store URL, issue description, optional page URL, theme version, and optional attachment. These details are used to understand your request and reply to you.

How requests are processed

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are sent to FormSubmit and forwarded to wukaicheng@iadlink.com. These services may process technical information as described in their own policies. This site does not add advertising or analytics scripts.

Keep sensitive details out

Do not submit passwords, payment details, customer records, or other sensitive personal data. Redact screenshots and share only what is needed to reproduce the issue.

Questions or removal requests

Email wukaicheng@iadlink.com to ask about your support information or request its removal, subject to applicable obligations.
